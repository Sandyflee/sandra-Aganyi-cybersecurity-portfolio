ABOUT ME

From Microbiology to Cybersecurity.

My first degree is in Microbiology, but I became increasingly interested in how organisations protect their systems, information and customers from technology-related risks.

I moved into cybersecurity through practical training, internships, risk-management programmes and independent projects. My work now sits mainly around GRC, IT risk, information security, vulnerability assessment, ISO/IEC 27001 and security awareness.

I also enjoy teaching. I have delivered cybersecurity and GRC training to beginners and early-career learners, and I am interested in making cybersecurity more practical and accessible for small and growing businesses.
